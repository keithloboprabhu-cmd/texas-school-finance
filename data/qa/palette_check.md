# Palette colour-vision check

Smallest CIE76 colour difference (Delta E) between classes after simulation. For ordered palettes the check is between adjacent classes; for the bivariate grid, between any two cells. Rough guide: Delta E > 10 is clearly distinguishable, 5-10 noticeable side by side, < 5 hard to tell apart.

| Palette | Pairs | Normal | Deuteranopia | Protanopia | Grayscale |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sequential (7) | adjacent | 22.3 | 16.4 | 17.0 | 10.4 |
| Diverging (7) | adjacent | 17.3 | 17.0 | 14.0 | 14.8 |
| Bivariate 3x3 | min | 16.2 | 14.2 | 9.7 | 1.4 |

The bivariate grid is not grayscale-safe (two cells can share a luminance); for print or grayscale use the Atlas single-metric view, whose palettes keep Delta E >= 10 between neighbouring classes.

Every map also offers 'View as table', hover cards print exact values, and legends label each class, so no reading depends on colour alone. Generated by scripts/palette_check.py.
