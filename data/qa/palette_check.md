# Palette color-blindness check

We simulate common types of color blindness and grayscale printing, then measure the smallest difference between any two colors that need to be told apart (CIE76 Delta E). For ordered scales we compare neighboring colors; for the two-measure grid we compare every pair. As a rough guide, above 10 is easy to tell apart, 5-10 is visible side by side, and below 5 is hard to see.

| Palette | Pairs | Normal | Deuteranopia | Protanopia | Grayscale |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sequential (7) | adjacent | 22.3 | 16.4 | 17.0 | 10.4 |
| Diverging (7) | adjacent | 17.3 | 17.0 | 14.0 | 14.8 |
| Bivariate 3x3 | min | 16.2 | 14.2 | 9.7 | 1.4 |

The two-measure grid doesn't work in grayscale, because two of its colors have the same lightness. For print or grayscale, use the Atlas's One measure view, where neighboring colors stay at least 10 apart.

Every map can also be shown as a table, hover cards print exact values, and legends label each color, so nothing depends on color alone. Generated by scripts/palette_check.py.
